Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I switch to solar energy?

+
Solar energy reduces your electricity bills, protects you from rising energy costs, and provides a clean, renewable source of power.

How much can I save with solar?

+
Savings depend on your electricity consumption, system size, and local utility rates. Most customers recover their investment within a few years and enjoy long-term savings.

How do I know what size solar system I need?

+
We analyze your electricity bills, roof space, and energy usage to recommend the most suitable system for your needs.

Will solar panels work during cloudy weather?

+
Yes. Solar Panel still generate electricity on cloudy days, although production is lower than on sunny days.

What happens during a power outage?

+
A standard grid-tied solar system automatically shuts down for safety. If you add battery storage, you can continue using stored electricity during outages.

How long do solar panels last?

+
Most high-quality solar panels have a lifespan of 25-30 years and continue producing electricity beyond that with slightly reduced efficiency.

Is maintenance required?

+
Solar systems require minimal maintenance. After every 10 days cleaning and routine inspections are usually enough to keep them operating efficiently.

How long does installation take?

+
Most residential installations are completed within 1-3 days, while commercial projects may take longer depending on their size.

Do you provide warranties?

+
Yes.We offer manufacturer warranties on solar panels, inverters, and installation workmanship for complete peace of mind.

Can solar increase my property value?

+
Lower electricity bill of the house and continuity of the electricity throught alternate source to help you to appreciate your property.

Do you offer financing or installment plans?

+
We can help you explore financing options to make solar more affordable.
