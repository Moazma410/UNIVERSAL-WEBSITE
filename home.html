Our Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted and innovative renewable energy company by delivering world-class solar engineering solutions, fostering lasting customer relationships, and contributing to a sustainable future powered by clean energy.

Our Mission

At *New Universal Solar & Trader*, our mission is to transform the way people consume energy by providing reliable, efficient, and cost-effective solar solutions tailored to every customer's needs. We are committed to engineering excellence, uncompromising quality, and continuous innovation.

We believe that our greatest strength lies in the trust of our customers. That's why we focus on building long-term relationships through honesty, transparency, professional expertise, and exceptional after-sales support. Every project we deliver reflects our dedication to customer satisfaction, environmental responsibility, and creating a brighter, greener future for generations to come.

Message from CEO

Every great achievement begins with trust.

At New Universal Solar & Trader, we believe that our greatest success is not measured by the number of solar systems we install, but by the lasting relationships we build with our customers. Every project is an opportunity to earn confidence, create value, and establish a partnership that extends far beyond installation.

As a BE Energy Engineering Specialist in Solar Engineering, with professional experience at Inverex, I have learned that true excellence comes from combining technical expertise with honesty, integrity, and exceptional customer care. These values are the foundation of everything we do.

Our commitment is simple—to provide innovative solar solutions, uncompromising quality, and dependable after-sales support while treating every customer with the respect and transparency they deserve. We are passionate about helping families and businesses embrace clean energy with confidence.

Thank you for placing your trust in New Universal Solar & Trader. We are honored to be your energy partner and remain committed to delivering excellence, building lasting relationships, and powering a brighter, more sustainable future together.
